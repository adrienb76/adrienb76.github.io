Last updated: June 2026

The Game does not require or request any sensitive hardware or software permissions on your device (such as access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, local files, or GPS location).

Because the Game collects absolutely no data, it is fully compliant with international children's privacy regulations, including the US COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and the European GDPR. The application is completely safe for minors.
